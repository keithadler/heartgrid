HeartGrid

Collect every heart, push the emeralds, open the door – then step through to the next room.
What is this?

HeartGrid is a puzzle game on a coordinate grid. Collect every heart, push emerald blocks (into water, to bridge it), dodge the ghosts and leave through the door. Twelve rooms, each needing more moves than the last. For every room, the fewest possible moves has been worked out and checked by computer, so you can guess it, try to match it, and see how close you got.
